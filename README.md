# Exploratory Sales and Product Analysis (2022–2023)

Data analysis project focused on exploring sales, pricing, and product behavior during the period 2022–2023.  
The analysis was conducted using **Tableau**, with emphasis on detecting seasonal patterns, price–demand relationships, and revenue concentration, aiming to generate actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze monthly revenue evolution.  
- Detect seasonality patterns and anomalies.  
- Evaluate the relationship between unit price and quantity sold.  
- Identify key products in terms of revenue and volume.  
- Propose pricing and portfolio optimization strategies.  

---

## 🛠️ Tools
- **Tableau** – Exploratory analysis and visualization.  

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Revenue drops consistently in **February and September** across both years.  
- **March** shows a strong recovery effect.  
- **November 2023** revenue is significantly lower compared to November 2022.  
- **Water** dominates beverage sales, highlighting strong price sensitivity.  
- **Ice Cream** and **Chocolate Cake** lead dessert sales.  
- Around **60% of revenue comes from only 8 products**, while sales volume is more evenly distributed.  

---

## 📈 Analyses and Findings

### 1️⃣ Monthly Revenue Evolution (2022–2023)
![Gráfico de Suscripciones](https://github.com/juanmcastineira/proyecto-3/blob/e1848966c78f0b70cf2024a7b1fb41f98cb8b388/images/ganancias%20por%20a%C3%B1o.png)
**Findings:**
- Recurrent revenue drops in February and September.  
- Significant sales increase in March, suggesting recovery after low-demand periods.  
- November 2023 shows a notable decline compared to November 2022.  

**Recommendations:**
- Investigate external factors (seasonality, holidays, climate).  
- Design promotional campaigns specifically for March and November.  

---

### 2️⃣ Quantity Sold vs. Unit Price
![](https://github.com/juanmcastineira/proyecto-3/blob/e1848966c78f0b70cf2024a7b1fb41f98cb8b388/images/cantidad%20por%20precio.png)

**Desserts:** Ice Cream and Chocolate Cake are the most sold items.  
**Drinks:** Water leads in volume, showing strong price sensitivity.  
**Main Dishes:** Mid-priced items concentrate most sales; Steak and Salmon show lower demand.  
**Side Dishes:** Side Salad stands out with high volume and low price.  
**Starters:** Nachos show low sales and relatively high price.  

**Recommendations:**
- Adjust pricing or improve perceived value (combos, presentation).  
- Consider reformulation or replacement of low-performing products.

### 4️⃣ Revenue by Product and Pareto Analysis
![Gráfico de Suscripciones](https://github.com/juanmcastineira/proyecto-3/blob/e1848966c78f0b70cf2024a7b1fb41f98cb8b388/images/ventas%20por%20producto.png)

**Findings:**
- ~60% of revenue comes from only 8 products, mainly main dishes.  
- Sales volume is more evenly distributed, indicating some products sell in high quantities but contribute less to revenue.  

**Implications:**
- Evaluate demand elasticity for high-impact products.  
- Simulate pricing scenarios:  
  - Reduce prices for main dishes to test if higher volume offsets margin loss.  
  - Apply moderate price increases to best-selling products to measure demand impact.

## ✅ Business Value
- **Seasonality detection** → better planning of promotions and inventory.  
- **Price–demand analysis** → supports pricing strategy adjustments.  
- **Revenue concentration (Pareto)** → helps optimize product portfolio and focus on high-impact items.

## 📌 Conclusion
The analysis reveals a clear relationship between **price and quantity sold**, with distinct behaviors across categories.  
Seasonality patterns and strong revenue concentration were identified, enabling concrete strategies for pricing, promotions, and portfolio optimization with direct business impact.
